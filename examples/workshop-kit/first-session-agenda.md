# A first modelling session: 90 minutes

**Aim:** leave with one question, about ten assets, their relationships, and a named owner for what comes next.

**Who to invite:** five to eight people. At least one operator, one person from maintenance or asset
management, one from automation or SCADA, and one who will own the result. Keep managers to one.

**Before the session:** send everyone [page 1, the ideas](../../docs/1-the-ideas.md)
(about five minutes to read). The person leading the types step checks whether a published standard already
defines the likely types, so the session is not spent searching.

**Bring:** the [blank template](model-template-blank.xlsx) on a shared screen (and the
[filled-in example](model-template.xlsx) to show what the result looks like), a process drawing of one area,
and a whiteboard.

| Time | What happens | Who leads | What you have at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Why we are here.** One slide or two sentences: we are writing down what our equipment is and how it connects, once, so every tool reads the same meaning | The sponsor | Agreement on the aim |
| 0:05 | **Pick one question.** For example: "when the wet well level is high, what should we look at first?" Write it at the top of the whiteboard | Operations | One question |
| 0:15 | **Name the types.** What kinds of thing does the question involve? Aim for five to ten. Use what the standards check before the session found | Automation | The Types sheet |
| 0:30 | **List the assets.** About ten that matter to the question. Give each a type and a parent | Asset management | The Assets sheet |
| 0:45 | **Draw the relationships.** On the whiteboard first, in plain sentences: "the pumps feed the forcemain". Then enter them | Operations | The Relationships sheet |
| 1:05 | **Add what is measured.** For each asset, what do we measure, and in what unit? | Automation | The Measurements sheet |
| 1:15 | **Read it back.** One person reads every relationship aloud. The operator says "yes", "no" or "it depends" | The owner | A corrected model |
| 1:25 | **Decide what happens next.** Who owns the file? Where is it kept? When do we meet again? | The sponsor | An owner, a place and a date |

## Tips

- **Stay on the one question.** When someone says "we should also add…", write it on a parking list.
- **Leave addresses for later.** The Bindings sheet can be filled in afterwards by the automation group. The
  first sheet counts the measurements still waiting.
- **Expect disagreement about names.** That is the session working. Write down what was decided and why.
- **Do not model everything.** A small model people trust is worth more than a large one nobody has checked.

## After the session

1. Clear every row that does not say "ok".
2. Put the file where the team can find it, with a version number and a date.
3. Fill in the [ownership worksheet](ownership-worksheet.md).
4. Pick the next question.
